Significance & Agriculture Impact

Mitigating Yield Losses

Global crop production suffers up to 40% annual loss due to plant pests and diseases. Early detection enables localized treatments before pathogen transmission spreads.

Targeted Pesticide Use

Instead of blanket spraying entire fields with chemical fungicides, farmers can pinpoint exact infection clusters, reducing chemical run-off and environmental damage.

Offline Edge Intelligence

By compiling models via TensorFlow.js, diagnosis occurs client-side in the browser, enabling smart farming in remote rural zones with poor internet access.

Target Audiences & Use Cases

Smallholder Farmers

Instantly identifies subtle symptoms without needing to hire expensive agricultural consultants or wait days for laboratory soil/plant diagnostics.

Botanical Researchers

Enables rapid automated field scoring and phenotypic disease tracking during crop breeding programs and resistance trials.

Hydroponics & Greenhouses

Integrates into automated camera rigs to continuously scan closed-loop greenhouse crops for early fungal outbreaks.

Exhibition Science & Math Panel

PhytoVision AI uses Transfer Learning on top of a MobileNet Architecture. MobileNet utilizes depthwise separable convolutions to reduce parameters while maintaining high feature extraction accuracy for mobile/browser platforms.
